# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme’s project management approach is designed to keep teams aligned while delivering value in predictable increments. Work follows a clear lifecycle of initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Projects begin with a one-pager that defines the problem, goals, success metrics, stakeholders, and early risks, then move into backlog planning with acceptance criteria, estimates, and a release outline.

Roles are intentionally defined to support accountability across the team. Project Managers coordinate delivery plans, schedules, and risk management; Product Managers own outcomes and prioritization; Developers implement and document solutions; QA and testing contributors validate quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders guide direction and approvals. This shared role model helps teams make decisions quickly without losing ownership clarity.

Communication is handled through a consistent cadence and transparent artifacts. Teams use daily standups and weekly delivery syncs to surface progress, blockers, and dependencies, while monthly stakeholder updates keep broader groups informed. Project boards, risk registers, and decision logs serve as the single source of truth for status, escalation, and cross-team coordination.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout delivery instead of being deferred to the end. CI pipelines are expected to run tests, linting, and security checks before merge, and major releases include smoke tests and manual validation of critical flows. Teams reinforce quality with small pull requests, clear Definitions of Done, and regular retrospectives that convert lessons learned into tracked improvement actions.
